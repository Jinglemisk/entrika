# muhtesem: English version and art gaps (proposal)

Step 1 of the mod plan (`plan.md`). The Turkish words and the Ottoman look already exist; this covers the English words and the missing or weak art. Nothing here is written into the game yet.

**Subtitle in English:** *Magnificent Century*, the show's international title. The game stays **Entrika**.

## Principles

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| People keep their Turkish names, spelled with Turkish letters. | Hürrem, Süleyman, Gülşah, Matrakçı Nasuh |
| A title English already has takes its English spelling. Turkish ş and ğ in a title make English readers say it wrong. | Paşa → Pasha, Ağa → Agha, Ferman → Firman |
| A title English doesn't have stays Turkish. | Sultan, Hatun, Kalfa, Efendi, Çelebi |
| Foreigners and the one Turk English already knows by another name take their English name. | Şarlken → Charles V, Tahmasp → Shah Tahmasp, Barbaros → Barbarossa |
| Anything a player must act on at a glance is translated. | Hizmet → Service, Hıyanet → Treason, every card and power name |
| Words that are the setting and that English readers half-know stay. | Harem, Divan, Kader |
| The manual's Glossary already names most terms in English. Reuse it unless a word is generic or collides. | Loyalist, Traitor, Influence, Decree, Objection |

## 4. Open questions

1. **Vizier or Minister?** I propose Vizier. It changes the most text, so it's the one to settle first.
2. **Retainer or Companion?** I propose Retainer.
3. **Plotting or Court** for step 3? I propose Plotting because "Court" also means the cast and the palace.
4. **Kader or Fate** for the deck and the step? I propose keeping Kader, with "Fate" in small type on the card back.
5. **English title spellings:** Pasha and Agha, but Sultan, Hatun, Kalfa, Efendi and Çelebi stay Turkish. Agreed? Or keep Paşa and Ağa as written?
6. **English names for the famous ones:** Charles V, Shah Tahmasp, Hayreddin Barbarossa, Prince Mustafa, Sultan Süleyman. Or keep Şarlken, Barbaros and Şehzade for flavor?
7. **Mother's Demand** (your example) or **The Valide's Command**? The second keeps the title, at the cost of a word English players won't know.
8. **The UI verb "seal".** The interface says "Mühürle" for locking in a hidden play, and Seal is also a card. English interface sentences are shared across mods, so I'd use "Lock in" there and keep Seal for the card. That's an interface string, not a mod word: who owns it?
9. **Turkish glosses on English phones?** The manual puts the Turkish word in grey beside each term. Should English cards do the same for kept words (Kader, Harem, Divan), or leave them bare?
10. **Flavor lines (step 2):** translate them all, or leave a few famous ones in Ottoman with an English line under them (Süleyman's "Zıllüllâhi fî'l-arz!": "The Shadow of God on Earth")?
11. **Ayas Pasha's art:** keep your image (source unknown) until a verified still turns up, or switch to the Kul emblem now?
12. **Counterplot's art:** OK to re-pick it, since the card was renamed and the still no longer fits?
