# Entrika: game screen, v2

The spec for the first playable's table, published as a clickable prototype (link in the report and in `plan.md` once Jingle accepts it). It replaces the v1 proposal in `ui/`, which Jingle rejected as generic. The page itself explains each step; this file holds what a builder needs and the page doesn't say.

## Files

- `index.html` is generated by `python3 ui/v2/src/build.py` from the parts in `src/` (page shell, CSS, script, the explanatory section). The block between `ART:BEGIN` and `ART:END` is pulled from `art/directions/index.html` and `art/terms.json` on every build, so when the art page changes, rebuild rather than editing that block. Edit `src/`, never `index.html`.
- `img/` holds copies of the art page's images. The Character cut-outs and Crisis full-art crops are still ungraded; the bake (`art/stills/bake.py`) should cover them before the build.

## Board geometry

- Everything tappable sits on a 734×372 board: the safe area of an 852×393 iPhone Pro in landscape (59 px each side, 21 px home indicator). The board is scaled uniformly into whatever safe area the phone reports (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`) and centred. At 932×430 it scales to 1.07.
- Floor, carpet glow, night wash and scrims are outside the board and bleed to the screen edges.
- The hand bleeds 21 px into the home-indicator zone, as in Hearthstone. Touches that start in that strip can trigger the iOS home gesture, so a card's drag handle is its upper part.
- Seats: ellipse centre (367, 212), radii 330×178, opponents spread over 146° (24.3° apart at 7 opponents, closer to the top with fewer). The arc starts at the viewer's left, so it rotates per phone.
- No scrolling in any game screen. Only the log scrolls, inside its own scroll.

## Interaction model

- **Asks.** Every decision is an ask addressed to one seat: `place`, `object`, `pick` (a player), `kader1`, `kader2`, `decree`, `power` (use/skip), `ferman`, `peek`. The addressed phone renders it; all other phones name the seat being waited on. The prototype's `ask()`/`answer()` are the seam: in the game, the relay delivers asks and answers.
- **Legal options come from the rules module.** The same functions (`playOptions`, `targetsFor`, `eligible`, the Loyalist duty check) decide which hand cards glow, which seats glow and which Kader card is locked. An illegal option is never shown as available: hand cards outside the open field are greyed, seats that can't be targeted dim, a Kader card the duty forbids is locked and shakes.
- **Card play.** Drag a card onto a glowing seat, or tap it (it lifts with a name ribbon) and tap the seat. Near a legal seat the dragged card shrinks into its landing slot. The placed card flies there and turns face down; tap it to take it back until you seal. One commit (Mühürle) or decline (Pas geç) per player; the result is a single ready tick for everyone else.
- **Kul recast at placement.** If the Kul can recast the selected card into an open-field card, a lapis Kul cartouche appears beside it; tapping it switches the card's identity, and the legal seats follow. A card that only works through the Kul starts recast and shows the Kul's face on its corner. The Kul pip greys out for the crisis once used, and the whole Kul is striped at 0 Nüfuz.
- **Powers in someone else's step** (Kulis, Göz Bebeği, Fısıltı, Himaye) open a use/skip slip on the holder's phone while the step waits. Limited powers show as pips in your corner: red base, blue Hırs power (locked with the Hırs icon until revealed), Kul recast. Used pips are grey and struck through; the seat sheet shows the same for other players.
- **Secrets.** Press and hold your portrait to see Taraf and Hırs (Hain players also see their fellow Hains). Long-press any card to read it at 258 px. Long-press a seat for that player's card, powers and your private şüpheli/güvenilir tag.
- **Pacing.** Süleyman's plaque is the only way a step closes; it stays dim with a reason (“5/7 hazır”) until the step can close. No timers.
- **Attention.** “Sıra sende” in coral under the step cartouche, gold glow on whatever you can touch, one sound and one buzz when your move begins. Visual cues are always on.

## Synced moments

Each runs the same script on every phone from a start time the relay sends. Durations as built:

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Crisis flip | card lifts from tray to 236 px (520 ms), turns (760 ms), holds ~1.7 s, returns (480 ms); then draw cards fly |
| Banner | unrolls from the centre (420 ms), holds ~0.9 s, folds into the step cartouche (420 ms) |
| Ceremony | tuğra, both Character cards rise (560 ms, 160 ms apart), hold 1.5 s, fly into the Vekil plates (520 ms) |
| Reveal | placed cards slide from the centre to their seats face down (480 ms, 90 ms apart), pause 650 ms, all turn together (700 ms); resolve one by one, each card grows 2.5× in front of its seat |
| Kader landing | played card to centre (560 ms), turn (700 ms), hold 1.2 s, arc into its niche (700 ms), tray jolts |
| Decree | sealed ferman drops, wax breaks, scroll unrolls (640 ms), holds 1.5 s; coin flies |
| Hırs reveal | completed Hırs cards turn face up for everyone; Favor pips unlock |

## Rules decisions made for online play

- An Objection's replacement must be a different player from the one removed (ruled 2026-11-13).
- Fısıltı applies to both the +1 and the −1 Hüküm (ruled 2026-11-13).
- Saray Dedikodusu, Mühürlü Evrak, Teftiş and Soruşturma open a private peek on the owner's phone; others see an eye mark on the card while it is open.
- Valide'nin Buyruğu asks its owner to pick the new Vekil when it resolves, not at placement.
- Two replacements on one Vekil resolve in seat order from Süleyman's left, as the manual says; the second is crossed out.

## Not in the scripted round

These need the same patterns and aren't shown: Çifte Oyun (the seal reads “1/2” until both cards are placed), Yön Değiştir (a use/skip slip at the reveal, then drag one card on you to another seat), Muhafız (use/skip after a Hıyanet is played), Sürgün, Haber and Hediye (tap the pip any time outside Kader, then pick a seat), Hazine Defteri, Kara Ahmed Paşa's four-card draw, and the Learning game mode.
