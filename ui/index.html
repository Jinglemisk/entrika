Entrika table layout

Every screen of a crisis, drawn at true CSS size inside a landscape iPhone 15 Pro (852×393, Home Screen web app). The frames use the art page's own card markup, tokens and fonts. Turkish strings are the proposed UI copy; notes are in English. The build agent should treat this page as the spec.

To judge it on a phone: open this page on the iPhone, turn it to landscape, and tap Open full screen on any frame. That screen then fills the real viewport with the real safe areas. The i button at the top-left shows the measured viewport and insets; the arrows step through screens.

    Parts and turn cues

    The building blocks at 1:1 on the table leather. The turn indicator is four layers that always agree: the cartouche names the step and who is acting; acting players' portraits pulse on the arc; when it's you, the cartouche turns vermilion, the gold rule above your bar lights and the action button pulses; legal targets get a dashed gold ring. Sound and vibration fire once, on the change to "your move".

    Screens

    One crisis, start to finish, with the same table throughout: crisis 4, Yeniçeri İsyanı (Divan). Each frame says whose phone it is. The story: Süleyman appoints Ayşe and Ece; Selin objects to Ece for free and puts Deniz in; at night Mert's Valide'nin Buyruğu replaces Deniz with Bora; Ayşe passes Hizmet and Hıyanet, Bora plays Hıyanet; it lands on Soruşturma.

    Overlays

    Popups over the table. Every overlay closes with a 44 px close button at the top-right and by tapping the dimmed table.

    Motion

    Motion carries information here: where a card came from, where it went, and that everyone saw the same thing at the same moment. Synced moments start from a server timestamp so every phone plays them together. Everything else is local.

    Ergonomics

    Two thumbs on a landscape phone reach the lower left and lower right comfortably, the middle band with a stretch, and the top centre only by shifting the grip. The layout puts frequent actions in the green zones and reading material at the top.
