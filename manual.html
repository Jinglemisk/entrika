Entrika

There's a traitor in the palace. Player manual for an unofficial fan game based on the TV series Muhteşem Yüzyıl.

Overview

  • 5–8players
  • ~30minutes
  • 12+ages
  • Unofficial fan game

Sultan Süleyman rules in public, and everyone knows he is loyal. Some of his courtiers are secret Traitors who want him dead.

Each round, a Crisis hits the palace. Süleyman appoints a First Minister and a Second Minister to handle it. They secretly decide its fate with the Kader deck. A Hizmet card helps the empire. A Hıyanet card brings the Sultan closer to death.

Everyone schemes with Intrigue cards, earns Influence from the Sultan, and unlocks powers along the way.

Loyalists win

when 4 Hizmet cards are on the Loyalty track.

Traitors win

when 5 Hıyanet cards are on the Treason track. The 5th space is Süleyman's death.

Nobody is eliminated. On the winning side, the player with the most Influence is the overall winner. Full rules: Tracks & winning.

Who you are

Every player except Süleyman has these pieces.

AllegianceSecretLoyalist or Traitor.
CompanionPublicA helper. It lets you play any Intrigue card as its specialty, once per crisis. It works only while you have at least 1 Influence. See Companions.
Intrigue handSecretUp to 3 cards. See Court & Intrigue.
InfluencePublicStar tokens, from 0 up. The most on the winning side wins overall. See Influence, Ambitions & Favor.

If you are Süleyman

You are public and always a Loyalist. You get no Allegiance card. You have no Companion, no Intrigue cards, no Influence and no Ambition. You are not in the Influence race: you win with the Loyalists.

Your job: appoint the Ministers, issue Decrees, and use your Ferman and the Treason track powers.

Setup

  1. Give the Süleyman card to one player, face up. A volunteer works well: the Sultan talks a lot.
  2. Deal every other player a random Character, face up.
  3. Deal Allegiance cards face down to everyone except Süleyman. Use the table below. Put any extra card back in the box unseen.
  4. Everyone looks at their Allegiance in secret. Traitors also see who the other Traitors are; the game shows them. If you are a Traitor and see nobody, you are alone.
  5. Each player except Süleyman draws 2 Companions, keeps 1 face up, and returns the other to the box.
  6. Each player except Süleyman takes 1 Influence and draws 2 Intrigue cards.
  7. Deal each player except Süleyman 1 Ambition, face down. Look at yours and keep it secret. Put the rest back in the box unseen.
  8. Shuffle the Kader deck and the Crisis deck. Both tracks start empty.

Learning game

For your first game, skip Companions, Ambitions and Favor powers. Play with Characters (Base powers only), Intrigue, Kader and Decrees. Leave the Wildcards out of the deal. The player with the most Influence on the winning side still wins overall. Ties share the win.

A Crisis, step by step

Play one Crisis after another until a side wins. Each crisis has six steps.

  1. 1

    Crisis

    Flip the top Crisis card and read it aloud. It opens a field for the crisis: Divan, Harem or Saray (both). See Fields. Şah Tahmasp's Cephe may change the field.

    Do its draw: each Crisis card says who draws 1 Intrigue card.

    The hand limit is 3: discard down if needed.

  2. 2

    Appoint

    Süleyman gives the First Minister marker and the Second Minister marker to two different players.

    He can appoint any player except himself and a player named by Sürgün.

    Then Objections, in public. Going clockwise from his left, each player except Süleyman may pay 1 Influence to object to one Minister. Süleyman must replace that Minister with another eligible player of his choice. The removed Minister draws 1 Intrigue card. You may object once per crisis at most, and not to your own seat. Şah Sultan's İtiraz happens here too.

  3. 3

    Court

    Played in waves. Each wave, every player except Süleyman privately plays one Intrigue card of an open field on any player but him, or passes. The cards flip together and take effect at once, and the next wave opens. There is no limit per crisis.

    A wave where nobody plays ends Court. Then Mantık and Nöbet cards show their owners what they learned, Mustafa may use Veliaht, and Süleyman may use his Ferman. See Court & Intrigue.

  4. 4

    Kader

    The First Minister draws 3 Kader cards, looks at them secretly, discards 1 face down, and passes the other 2 face down to the Second Minister. Under Defterdar, they draw 4 and discard 2.

    The Second Minister discards 1 face down and plays the other face up.

    Nobody talks, shows cards or uses a power during this step, unless the power says so. See The Kader deck.

  5. 5

    Result

    Put the played card on its track: Hizmet on the Loyalty track, Hıyanet on the Treason track.

    If a Hıyanet lands on a power space, Süleyman gets that power now. See Tracks & winning.

    Then Divan Nüfuzu pays and each Kefalet settles. Check for a win. Then the Ministers may defend themselves. They may claim anything, but nobody may show hidden cards.

  6. 6

    Decree

    If Hizmet was played, Süleyman must give +1 Influence to one of the two Ministers.

    If Hıyanet was played, he must take 1 Influence from one of them. Influence can't go below 0. A player who loses Influence draws 1 Intrigue card for each one lost. A player under Çeşnigir or Himaye loses nothing.

    A Minister under Nazar gains or loses 2 instead of 1.

    Then reveal any completed Ambitions, return the Minister markers, and start the next crisis.

The Kader deck

The Kader deck has 18 cards: 7 Hizmet and 11 Hıyanet. Both kinds have the same back.

No duty

Either Minister may discard or play any card, whatever their side. An honest pair can still fail: if the First Minister draws 3 Hıyanet, the Second Minister gets 2 Hıyanet and has no choice.

«Sultanım, yemin ederim size yardım etmek istedim, fakat yapamadım!»

My Sultan, I swear I wanted to help you, but I couldn't!

Discard pile

Put discarded Kader cards on a face-down discard pile. When fewer than 3 Kader cards are left to draw (4 when the First Minister is under Defterdar), shuffle the discard pile into the deck. Cards on the tracks stay there.

Example

First Minister Pargalı draws Hizmet, Hıyanet, Hıyanet. Pargalı is a Traitor, so he discards the Hizmet and passes two Hıyanet. Second Minister Hatice must play a Hıyanet.

Pargalı later says: "I drew three Hıyanet, I swear!" Hatice knows he passed her two. Was he lying, or unlucky?

Court & Intrigue

Talk is public

Anyone may talk, accuse, promise or lie at any time. Always say it out loud, to the whole table. No private conversations. Only Rüstem's Kulis breaks this rule.

Waves

Court is played in waves. Each wave:

  1. Every player except Süleyman privately plays one Intrigue card of an open field on any player but Süleyman, or passes. You may play on yourself, unless the card says another player. Mühürlü Evrak is played on nobody. A card that needs a Minister goes only on a current Minister, and Defterdar only on the current First Minister.
  2. The cards flip together and take effect at once. See All at once.
  3. Players draw for what the wave cost them, and may play those cards in the next wave.

Passing in one wave doesn't stop you playing in the next, and there is no limit per crisis: one card a wave, for as many waves as there are. Anything once per crisis stays once per crisis, not once per wave. A player under Sükût sits out the next wave.

A wave where nobody plays ends Court. Then each Mantık and Nöbet shows its owner what it learned, Mustafa may use Veliaht, Süleyman may use his Ferman, anyone they remove draws, and the Kader step begins.

On phones

A flipped Crisis, each flipped wave and the Kader result stay on every phone until every player, Süleyman too, presses Devam.

With timers on (the default; off in the learning game), a wave times out as a pass, and an Objection turn, a power offer, Yön Değiştir and the Ferman time out as not used. Appointments, replacements, the Decree, Kader, discards and Devam are never timed: the talk is the game.

Who sees what

  • Cards are public, owners are not. When a wave flips, every phone shows each card, its target and what it did, but never who played it. A recast shows only as the card it was played as. Your Companion's used mark shows on your phone only.
  • Only some cards name an owner. Mantık and Nöbet tell their owner who played what. A Kefalet's gain or loss is public, so it can give its owner away.
  • Using a power is public; what it shows you is secret. Every phone gets a banner naming who used it, and what the power shows goes to your phone only. Two powers wait or stay quiet so they don't name a card's owner: Çifte Oyun is announced only when Court ends, without saying which wave, and Plus Ultra is never announced.
  • Some choices are made out of sight. While the owner of a Valide'nin Buyruğu picks the new Minister, the other phones show the flipped wave and "yeni Vekil seçiliyor", with no seat marked. Gözcü and the Nöbet pick are decided in a pause everyone waits through.
  • Hands are secret, including how many cards you hold. See Hand limit and discards.
  • What is still to come shows on the seat. A card or power waiting to work, such as a Mühür, a Sükût or a Teftiş, shows under its player until it has worked. Every Influence change shows on the seat, with its amount and reason in the log.

Fields

Every Intrigue card belongs to a field, Harem or Divan, shown on the card. Divan cards act on the Minister seats; Harem cards act on Influence and secrets.

Each Crisis card opens a field for the crisis, unless Cephe picks another. On a Divan crisis you may place only Divan cards, on a Harem crisis only Harem cards, and on a Saray crisis either. A card outside the open field does nothing, unless it is recast into a card of the open field (see Companions).

Cards only interact with cards of their own field. A Harem Oyunbozan cancels only Harem cards on its player.

All at once

Cards have no resolve order. When a wave flips:

  1. Reveal powers come first, such as Yön Değiştir.
  2. Then every card in the wave takes effect at once.
    • Protections work from the flip of their own wave and last the crisis. Oyunbozan cancels the other cards of its field on its player, Mühür keeps its Minister in the seat, and Çeşnigir turns a negative Influence total into 0. Cards that already took effect stay.
    • A card that needs a Minister checks for one at the flip. If its player was a Minister then, it works, even if another card in the wave moves them.
    • Owners who choose, such as the new Minister for Valide'nin Buyruğu, choose one at a time, clockwise from Süleyman's left. For Azil, Süleyman chooses in its owner's turn. If two cards would replace the same Minister, the first applies and the other does nothing. The other phones don't show who is choosing; see Who sees what.
    • Each player's İftira losses from the wave are added up and applied once.
  3. Then draws: 1 Intrigue card for each Influence lost and for each Minister seat lost.
Example

Pargalı is First Minister and Hatice is Second. In the first wave, Pargalı gets a Valide'nin Buyruğu and a Mühür. Protections work from their own wave's flip, so the Mühür wins and Pargalı keeps his seat. In the second wave, Hatice gets an Azil, and Süleyman puts Rüstem in her seat. Hatice draws 1 Intrigue card at the end of the wave and can strike back in the next one.

Eligible

A player is eligible for a Minister seat if they:

  • aren't Süleyman,
  • aren't the other current Minister, and
  • weren't named by Sürgün for this crisis.

"Appointed" means holding a Minister seat at the Kader step, however you got there.

Companions

When you play an Intrigue card, you may play it as your Companion's specialty instead. It counts as the card it is played as, field included, so you may recast a card from outside the open field. When you recast as Oyunbozan, name its field. Every Companion can do this once per crisis. The wave shows only the card it was played as, so a recast doesn't give you away. Your Companion works only while you have at least 1 Influence; see Companions. A recast that would be illegal for the card it is played as does nothing, for example a card on a player who isn't a Minister, played as Mühür.

Example

Your Companion is Matrakçı Nasuh. On a Harem crisis, your only card is a Mühür. You play it on another player and recast it as Mantık, a Harem card.

Drawing Intrigue cards

You draw 1 Intrigue card when:

  • the Crisis card says so,
  • you lose Influence (1 card for each Influence lost), from the Decree, İftira or Kefalet, or
  • you are removed from a Minister seat, by an Objection, Valide'nin Buyruğu, Azil, Veliaht, Ferman or any other effect.

Spending Influence on an Objection is not losing it. At 0 Influence there is nothing to lose, so you don't draw.

Hand limit and discards

Your Intrigue hand limit is 3 at all times, for everyone.

Your hand is secret, including how many cards you hold. Nobody else sees when you discard down to the limit.

Played and discarded Intrigue cards go on a face-down discard pile. When the Intrigue deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to make a new deck.

Influence, Ambitions & Favor

Influence

You start with 1 Influence. You can't go below 0. There is no maximum. At 0, your Companion turns face down; see Companions. Influence is public.

Gain

  • Süleyman's Decree: +1, or +2 under Nazar
  • Kefalet on Hizmet: +1
  • Some powers: Divan Nüfuzu, Faiz, Feragat, Ocak Sevgisi Q1

Lose

  • Süleyman's Decree: −1, or −2 under Nazar
  • İftira: −1
  • Kefalet on Hıyanet: −1
  • Each loss: draw 1 Intrigue

Spend

  • An Objection: 1

On the winning side, the player with the most Influence is the overall winner. See Overall winner.

Ambitions

At setup, everyone except Süleyman is dealt 1 Ambition, face down. Keep it secret. Events from the first crisis count.

Favor

Complete your Ambition. At the end of that crisis, reveal it to unlock your Character's Favor power once per game.

  1. Ambition dealt at setup
  2. Complete it
  3. Reveal at end of crisis
  4. Favor power unlocked

Tracks & winning

Loyalty track
  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4Loyalists win
Treason track
  1. 1No power
  2. 2Soruşturma
  3. 3Hazine Defteri
  4. 4Ferman refresh
  5. 5Süleyman dies

Loyalty track, 4 spaces. The 4th Hizmet means Loyalists win.

Treason track, 5 spaces. When a Hıyanet lands on a space, Süleyman immediately gets that space's power:

  1. Nothing.
  2. Soruşturma (investigation). Süleyman names an investigator, not himself. The investigator secretly looks at one other player's Allegiance card, then may say whatever they want about it.
  3. Hazine Defteri (treasury ledger). Before the next crisis, Süleyman names a player, not himself. That player secretly looks at the top 3 Kader cards, puts them back in the same order, and may say anything about them.
  4. Ferman refresh. Süleyman may use his Ferman again, even if he already used it. He never has more than one Ferman ready.
  5. Süleyman dies. Traitors win.

Overall winner

At game end, everyone reveals their Allegiance. On the winning side, the player with the most Influence is the overall winner. Süleyman is not in the Influence race; he wins with the Loyalists.

If players tie, whoever completed their Ambition wins. If they are still tied, they share the win.

Characters

Each Character has two powers. The Base power is once per game and ready from the start. The Favor power is once per game and locked until you complete an Ambition. Exceptions are marked.

Announce a power when you use it. Using a power never counts as playing a card. A power with no stated timing can be used at any time except during the Court and Kader steps. A power with a timing is offered to its owner when it applies, and only when it could change something.

The court is set in 1534 to 1536, while Pargalı is alive and Mustafa is grown. Seven courtiers serve it. Three Wildcards are outsiders who never sat in Süleyman's Divan, dealt like any other Character.

Intrigue cards

37 cards in 14 kinds: 19 Divan, 18 Harem. Cards played in the same wave take effect at once; see All at once. "This player" is the player the card is played on, never Süleyman.

Oyunbozan

Protection

Cancel every other Intrigue card of its field played on this player, whoever played it. It lasts the crisis: it also cancels such cards in later waves. Cards that already took effect stay. Oyunbozan cards don't cancel each other.

Crises

11 Crisis cards. Flip one at the start of each crisis and read it aloud. It opens a field (see Fields) and says who draws. Süleyman never draws.

"Last crisis's Ministers" are the players who held the seats at its Kader step; in the first crisis, nobody. If players tie for the least Influence, Süleyman picks one of them to draw.

Quick reference

A crisis

  1. 1CrisisFlip a Crisis: it opens a field and says who draws.
  2. 2AppointSüleyman picks the First Minister and Second Minister. Then Objections, clockwise.
  3. 3CourtIn waves: each player plays one Intrigue card of an open field, or passes. Each wave flips and takes effect at once. A wave with no cards ends it; then Mantık and Nöbet, Veliaht, Ferman.
  4. 4KaderFirst Minister draws 3 (4 under Defterdar), passes 2. Second Minister plays 1.
  5. 5ResultThe card goes on its track. Süleyman gets any power. Kefalet settles. Check for a win.
  6. 6DecreeHizmet: +1 Influence to a Minister. Hıyanet: −1 from one. Under Nazar, 2 either way.

Limits

  • One Intrigue card per wave, of an open field; no limit per crisis
  • Intrigue hand limit 3
  • Influence from 0 up, no maximum
  • Companion recast once per crisis; face down at 0 Influence
  • Objection costs 1 Influence, once per crisis each
  • Ambition dealt at setup
  • Süleyman never serves and never holds Intrigue
  • Draw 1 Intrigue when you lose Influence or a Minister seat
  • Win at 4 Hizmet or 5 Hıyanet. Most Influence on the winning side wins overall

Decided by Jingle

  • Süleyman is public and always loyal (the Bang! sheriff), and he appoints the Ministers.
  • Süleyman's Decree gives or takes Influence.
  • Completing an Ambition unlocks a second power.
  • Companions recast Intrigue cards. Jingle's own idea: "Sümbül: play any Intrigue as Saray Dedikodusu".
  • A Secret Hitler-style Kader chain («Sultanım, yemin ederim…»).
  • The Treason track ends in Süleyman's death.
  • An uncertain Traitor count.
  • The Harem/Divan idea is liked. It became Fields in v0.6.

New in v0.4

  • Hidden senders. Court is a night phase: eyes closed, Süleyman calls each player in turn and each places one card. Everyone except Süleyman holds a Boş Mektup to place instead of an Intrigue card. Only cards that make their owner choose or look are claimed. Powers are announced when used.
  • Objection is public and happens at the end of the Appoint step, once per player per crisis. Mühür no longer stops it.
  • Petition is removed. Çifte Oyun now places two cards in one night.
  • Ambitions are dealt at setup, one per player except Süleyman. The Influence threshold is gone.
  • Influence starts at 1 and runs from 0 to 3. (v0.6: no maximum.)
  • On the winning side, the player with the most Influence is the overall winner. Süleyman is not in the race. No special title.

New in v0.5

  • Intrigue intake. The flat draw of 1 per crisis is gone. You draw 1 Intrigue card when you lose Influence, when you are removed from a Minister seat, and when the Crisis card says so. Crisis cards are no longer flavor only.
  • No term limits. Süleyman may appoint anyone except himself; Sürgün still blocks. This also settles running out of eligible players at 5 players.
  • Gözde'nin Hediyesi stays anonymous and says "not you". Jingle accepts that this can't be enforced.
  • Hil'at, a Loyalty track reward where Süleyman gives +1 Influence to any player: rejected.

New in v0.6

  • Crisis draw ties. Where a Crisis says the player with the least Influence draws, Süleyman picks one of the tied players and only that player draws (Haremde Skandal, Irakeyn Seferi). On a players-at-0 crisis all of them still draw.
  • Companion at 0 Influence: face down, no effect. Face up again as soon as you gain Influence, at no cost.
  • No Influence cap: it runs from 0 up. The tie-break for the overall winner stays.
  • Fields. Every Intrigue card is Harem or Divan, shown on the card. Each Crisis opens Divan, Harem or Saray (both), and only cards of an open field may be placed that night; Boş Mektup always can. Cards only interact with cards of their own field. Placement is unchanged: there is no board.

New in v0.7

  • The setting is 1534 to March 1536: the only years with both a grown Mustafa and a living Pargalı.
  • Characters: Mustafa and Barbaros join the court. Gritti, Şarlken and Şah Tahmasp are Wildcards, always in the deal, left out of the learning game. Malkoçoğlu becomes a Companion; his Muhafız retires and his Gözcü goes to Barbaros.
  • Every power should be worth using on either side, and every Character has one Loyalist and one Traitor motive line, shown with your Allegiance.
  • Companions: Malkoçoğlu replaces Lokman Ağa, İskender Çelebi replaces Kara Ahmed Paşa, Firuze Hatun replaces Piri Mehmet Paşa. Their powers are unchanged.
  • Crises: Sınırda İsyan becomes Irakeyn Seferi. Isabella Fortuna joins as a third Saray crisis.
  • No power without a stated timing can be used during Court, because placement on phones is private and simultaneous.
  • No resolve order. Fields keep Divan and Harem cards apart, so the 1 to 10 order only settled a few clashes. Four rules cover them: protection cards apply first, a card checks for its Minister at the flip, owners choose in seat order, and Influence changes add up. Draws wait until after the Ferman. See All at once.
  • Court is played in waves, up to 2 Intrigue cards each per crisis. A deposed Minister draws and can strike back in the next wave. Oyunbozan (then Akıl Hocası) lasts the crisis, like Mühür and Saray Tabibi. The night, the Boş Mektup and claims are gone: on phones, nobody sees who played what anyway. Playtests move to the browser build, because a paper table can't hide who played what across waves.
  • Waves details, approved by Jingle: Çifte Oyun is two cards in one wave, within your 2. Esma Hatun's returned card still counts toward your 2. Saray Tabibi turns a negative wave total into 0. A Mühür and a Valide'nin Buyruğu on the same Minister in one wave: the Mühür wins, because protections work from their own wave's flip.

New in v0.9

  • Kul and Hırs pass (2026-11-18), P1 to P12 of the Hırs and Kul review taken. Every Companion recasts a card, once per crisis. The four special powers became cards: İskender Çelebi plays Defterdar, Firuze Hatun plays Nazar, Lokman Ağa plays Nöbet and Gül Ağa plays Sükût. The hand limit is 3 for everyone. Defterdar and Nöbet are Divan, Nazar and Sükût Harem, 2 copies each: 37 cards, 19 Divan and 18 Harem.
  • Casus is renamed Mantık and Akıl Hocası is renamed Oyunbozan. Sümbül Ağa and Matrakçı Nasuh swap cards: Matrakçı plays Mantık, and Sümbül plays Oyunbozan and picks its field.
  • Ambitions the holder can chase. New goals for Padişahın Takdiri (be a Minister when Hizmet is played), Gözden Düşmek (lose Influence in any real way), Ortak Kader (a Mühür or Kefalet of yours takes effect on a Minister), Divanda Nüfuz (your Valide'nin Buyruğu or Azil removes a Minister), Yükselen Yıldız (be a Minister at Kader in 2 crises), Saray Hazinesi (play 3 cards in one crisis) and Kulisçi (cards on 3 other players over the game). Kulak Misafiri adds Nöbet. Recasts count for every goal.
  • Rulings for the new cards: Defterdar goes only on the First Minister, and several on one Minister still mean 4 cards. Several Nazar on one Minister still mean 2; a −2 draws as many cards as were lost, and Çeşnigir or Himaye stop the whole loss. Mantık and Sükût can't go on yourself. A silenced player is passed with no sign to the table; their own phone says why. Nöbet offers only cards other players put on its player, a recast shows as the card it was played as, and a card moved by Yön Değiştir counts for its final target.
  • Who sees what (2026-11-19), from the feedback audit. A Kul recast is anonymous: the wave shows the card as played, and the Companion's used mark shows on its owner's phone only. Çifte Oyun is announced when Court ends, without saying which wave, and Plus Ultra is never announced. A Valide'nin Buyruğu's owner picks unseen while the other phones show the flipped wave. Mühürlü Evrak has no target, so the wave doesn't name its owner; no Oyunbozan reaches it and Yön Değiştir can't move it.
  • The rules each phone follows: anything waiting on a seat shows under it, every public Influence change shows with its amount and reason, a modifier such as Nazar or Defterdar is named on the screen it changes, and every secret look shows the cards or faces first, then one sentence starting with its source. A paid Objection shows its −1 but still isn't a loss for Gözden Düşmek.

Proposed, awaiting Jingle's OK

Nothing is waiting. The browser game plays every earlier proposal that still applies, so playtests now confirm or overrule them:

  • No team vote. A public Objection costs 1 Influence, and spending it is not losing it.
  • Süleyman holds no Intrigue cards. 5–8 players. Tracks of 4 and 5.
  • A tie for most Influence on the winning side goes to whoever completed their Ambition; still tied, they share the win.
  • Hazine Defteri, and a Ferman refresh that doesn't stack.
  • The edge-case defaults: the Intrigue discard pile, "appointed" means holding the seat at Kader, Sürgün blocks every way into a seat, using a power never counts as playing a card, and a recast that would be illegal does nothing.
  • The Crisis draw lines and fields, the learning game, Barbaros's Gözcü and a Companion at 0 Influence.
  • The Favor powers, Companions and Intrigue cards as they stand after the v0.9 pass.

The rest left the game with the rules they belonged to: Mihrimah's Hediye, Piri Mehmet Paşa's power, the resolve order, Divan Sırası swaps, claims and the Boş Mektup.

Watch list

  • No term limits. Süleyman can keep a trusted pair indefinitely. That can freeze other players out of the Influence race and makes Pargalı's Divan Nüfuzu stronger. Dial if playtests show it: not the same pair twice in a row. Jingle\'s view: Süleyman banking on one pair either wins big or loses big, so it corrects itself.
  • Kader 7/11. Count the winners over about 10 games. 7 or more Loyalist wins: try 6/12. 7 or more Traitor wins: try 7/10.
  • Court anonymity. Mantık, Lokman Ağa's Nöbet, Sezgi and Kefalet's public result all tell players who played what, and Matrakçı Nasuh can recast Mantık every crisis. Sümbül Ağa's Oyunbozan, in the field she picks, can shut a Mantık or Nöbet out of its player. Check that waves still feel anonymous now that every Kul recasts.
  • Defterdar and Sükût. Both are new, and İskender Çelebi and Gül Ağa can recast them every crisis. Watch whether a 4-card Kader draw or a silenced wave decides crises too often.
  • Azil and Objections. Do players stop paying for Objections once they hold an Azil?
